## Question

Run once, unchanged, on rivers that played no part in developing or correcting it, do the package's prediction intervals hold at their stated levels, for days, 7-day and 30-day means, in later years and in held-out years: as they are, and widened by the conformal margins?

## Method

30 USGS stream gauges in New England and the Mid-Atlantic (hydrologic regions 01 and 02), chosen by the rule in data/usa_northeast/download.py before any model was run on them: discharge on every day of 2011-2023, at least 300 daily water temperatures in at least 5 of the years 2011-2020 and at least 300 in 2021-2023, at most 30 stations drawn at random. Air temperature from Daymet, (tmax + tmin) / 2 at the gauge. Version 8, NSE, Crank-Nicolson, the authors' parameter ranges and the calibration Qmedia, as V19's Swiss runs.

- (A) Later years: DE-MCMC with the default least-squares likelihood and rho (32 walkers, run until converged, at most 100000 steps, as V18) on 2011-2020, then a FORWARD run of 1000 series from the chain for 2021-2023 with forward_options.conformal_margins set to the margins of (B).
- (B) Held-out years: the package's leave-one-year-out cross-validation of 2011-2020 (DE, every year but the first held out once), which writes cv_conformal_margins.csv; each held-out year is judged without a margin and with a margin from the river's other held-out years only (inside_after).
- (C) For each predicted period, its mean error against what the error model allows (z, as V18 part C). Shares are pooled over stations, weighted by the number of values; 95% confidence intervals resample stations (cluster bootstrap).

## Pass criterion

Fixed in this file before the check was first run, with the method unchanged since V19. With the conformal margins:

- (A) for the stations whose DE-MCMC converged, pooled, the share of later-year days inside the interval is within the accepted range at 50%, 80%, 90%, 95% (a miss rate between half and 1.5 times the stated one), and the share of 7-day and of 30-day means inside the 90% interval is between 85% and 95%;
- (B) pooled over every held-out year, days, 7-day and 30-day means inside the 80%, 90%, 95% intervals are within the accepted range. These are V19's criteria. The intervals without margins are judged by the same criteria (V18's), and the outcome is reported in the summary but does not decide the result: the margins are what the package recommends where cross-validation shows intervals that hold too rarely. The 99% intervals, single stations, unconverged stations and summer are reported, not judged.

## What this means

These rivers were chosen by a fixed rule (data/usa_northeast/download.py) and the check was written, with its criterion, before any model was run on them; the package was not changed between V19 and this run. They differ from both earlier regions: lowland and upland rivers of a humid continental climate, many small, several urban or regulated.
