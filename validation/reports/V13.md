# V13. Reproduces the 2015 published results

[Validation summary](../REPORT.md) · pyair2stream 0.5.0, commit 66372cd, 2026-10-09 01:27 UTC · run time 1.0 minutes

**Result: PASS.**

(A) With RK4, 15 of 15 parameter sets reproduce both published RMSE values within their rounding (largest difference 0.0058 °C); with Crank-Nicolson the differences reach 0.323 °C. (B) DE with RK4 fits the calibration years at least as well as the published parameters in 15 of 15 cases, and returns the published parameters (each within 1% of its range) in 15 of 15.

## Question

The first air2stream paper (Toffolon and Piccolroaz, 2015) published its own parameters and errors for the same three Swiss rivers, for versions 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8. Given those parameters, does pyair2stream reproduce the published errors, and with which numerical scheme? Does its own calibration with that scheme fit at least as well?

## Method

The 2015 parameters (Table 1 of the paper's supplementary information) and calibration and validation RMSE and NSE (Table 2) were transcribed into data/switzerland/published/Toffolon_Piccolroaz_ERL2015.csv.

- (A) Each of the 15 parameter sets is simulated with RK4 and with Crank-Nicolson (CRN) over the calibration and validation periods of the data distributed with the original code (Mentue 2002-2009 / 2010-2012, Rhône 1984-2004 / 2005-2013, Dischmabach 2003-2009 / 2010-2012), with each period's own Qmedia as the original program computes it (as in V2), and its RMSE and NSE are computed over the measured days. The published values are rounded: RMSE to 2 decimals and parameters to 3. The effect of the parameters' rounding is bounded by simulating 100 parameter sets that each differ from the published one by up to 0.0005 in every parameter. The paper's Table 1 gives the Rhône's periods as 1984-2003 / 2004-2013; that split is scored too.
- (B) Each version is calibrated by DE with RK4 (RMS objective, the authors' parameter ranges, the calibration years).

## Pass criterion

(A) With RK4, all 30 published RMSE values are reproduced to within 0.005 °C (their rounding) plus the largest effect of rounding the parameters. (B) DE with RK4 fits each calibration period no worse than the published parameters do (their RMSE with RK4, + 0.002 °C). Whether DE returns the published parameters is reported. CRN and the stated Rhône split are reported, not judged.

## What this means

The paper's Table 1 gives the Rhône's periods as 1984-2003 and 2004-2013. With that split the published errors are missed by up to 0.013 °C, also by versions 3 and 5, which do not use discharge; with the split of the data distributed with the code (1984-2004 and 2005-2013) they are reproduced. The 2015 results were therefore computed on the distributed split, and the periods in the table are a misprint.

The 2015 parameters belong to the RK4 scheme and the 2016 parameters (V2) to Crank-Nicolson: each set reproduces its paper's errors only with its own scheme. Parameters are specific to the scheme they were calibrated with (V2 part E, V6). Together, V2 and V13 reproduce 60 published errors from two papers, with two schemes, on the same data.
